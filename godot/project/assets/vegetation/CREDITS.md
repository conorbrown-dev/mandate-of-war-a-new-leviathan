# Vegetation credits

Every tree and bush is generated by EZ-Tree. `tools/build_near_trees.gd` grows
the meshes in `near/` (drawn as real models close to the camera), and
`tools/bake_tree_impostors.gd` renders the same meshes into the impostor
atlases in `impostors/` (drawn everywhere else). Both are rebuilt from the
files in this repository; nothing needs downloading.

| What | Source | Authors | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tree generator (`vegetation/ez_tree/ez_tree_generator.gd`) and species presets (`vegetation/ez_tree/presets/`) | [EZ-Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree), by way of its [Godot port](https://github.com/leomclaughlin4/ez-tree-godot-port), adapted for Mandate of War | Daniel Greenheck; Godot port by GitHub user leomclaughlin4 | MIT (`vegetation/ez_tree/LICENSE`) |
| Leaf textures (`ez_tree/leaves/`: ash, aspen, oak, pine) | EZ-Tree | Daniel Greenheck | MIT |
| Bark textures (`ez_tree/bark/`: Bark001, Bark003, Bark006, Bark012, Bark014; colour, normal and roughness) | [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/list?q=bark) | Lennart Demes | CC0 |
| Near meshes (`near/`) and impostor atlases (`impostors/`) | Generated from the above | — | Follow their sources |

The variants: five pines (`pine_a`–`pine_e`), three each of oak, ash and aspen,
two young pines and three bushes. Seeds and colour changes are listed in
`tools/build_near_trees.gd`.

The MIT licence asks that its copyright notice travels with the code; it is kept
in `vegetation/ez_tree/LICENSE`. CC0 needs no attribution; it is recorded here
so the source can be found again.

The earlier Poly Haven forest (firs, Scots pines, island trees, saplings,
shrubs and searsia) has been replaced. Its `pine_a`–`pine_c` atlases had the
same names as the new EZ-Tree pines and were overwritten by the new bake.
