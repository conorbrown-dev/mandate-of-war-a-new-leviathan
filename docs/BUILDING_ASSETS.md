# Civilian building model sources

The Broken Strait scenery uses these original GLB files from Poly Pizza. The
game scales them uniformly to the authored height, rests the mesh bounds on the
sampled terrain, and places them only on relatively flat land. They are scenery
and navigation obstacles, not a separate economy system.

The CC BY warehouse credit appears in the game's F2 **Audio & Credits** panel.
The creator and original model page must remain credited with distributions.
The warehouse has a visibly damaged, open roof; it is placed as war-worn
civilian scenery. The office tower is deliberately spare, with muted color
and no exaggerated silhouette. The water tower represents municipal utility;
drinking water stays within Materials as requested, with no new resource bar.

The user also suggested [Sketchfab's downloadable architecture gallery](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/categories/architecture?date=week&features=downloadable&sort_by=-likeCount).
Its most relevant recent industrial-building study at the time of review was
[99.3k triangles and CC BY](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/industrial-building-architectural-study-98907141519e451e8199694a893061a5),
large for repeated RTS scenery. The gallery's downloadable filter does not
itself define reuse rights; each model needs an individual license check.
