# 3D model audit and replacement sources

Audited 2026-11-23 from the repository (`godot/project/visuals/visual_definitions.json`,
`data/provenance/`, `docs/BUILDING_ASSETS.md`, `docs/POWER_ASSETS.md`,
`godot/project/credits.json`, `godot/project/ui/components/audio_settings.gd`).
Nothing was opened in Godot, and no licence was checked at its source except the
web results listed at the end.

The rule (`CLAUDE.md`): every sourced model must be royalty free for commercial use,
and each one must be credited in the game.

## Result

| Group | Models | Licence recorded | Credited in game | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recon planes (Farwatch MQ-9, Longsight U-2, Highwatch SR-71) | 3 | CC BY 4.0, with author and URL | Yes (`credits.json`) | OK |

## The unit models that fail

All 13 come from a "user-provided reference pack" that `reference_models/CREDITS.md` says
has no recorded licences, and `data/provenance/reference_model_mapping.json` calls
"temporary", "prototype_donor" art. Several are named after real vehicles and game-mod
packs, which is a copyright risk as well as a credit gap.

"Strike Fighters" is a flight-sim series with a large community mod scene. Assets from
those packs are normally not redistributable. Treat the whole group as unusable for a
commercial release until each is traced to its real author and licence, or replaced.

Missing outright: the Breakwater Patrol Boat has no visual id. The destroyer and carrier
models are marked inactive in the mapping (carrier donor, no destroyer reference).

Other findings:
- Repository weight: `reference_models/` is 262 MB, `nature/trees/` 151 MB, `cc0/` 51 MB
  (a single 34 MB `tank_battle_bg.glb`). Much of it is unused donor art.
- `visual.nature.tree.prototype` still points at `downloaded_assets/trees/oak_tree*.glb`,
  which does not exist in the repo, and at three `oak_tree_user_*.glb` files of unknown
  licence. The EZ-Tree system is what normally draws trees; confirm the old path is dead
  and delete the files.
- The `cc0/` folder's `provenance_source_id`s (for example `oga_freeciv_units`) point to
  OpenGameArt collections, but no author, page URL or licence text is recorded per model.
  Only the naval seeds are referenced by a visual definition.

## Replacement sources

Only sources whose licence can be checked at the model's own page. Verify each before
use; the web results below are search summaries, not legal confirmation. Record every
model in `credits.json` (if credit is needed) and in a `CREDITS.md` beside it, with the
author, page URL, licence and the SHA-256 of the stored file, as `BUILDING_ASSETS.md` does.

**Best option: make the units ourselves.** `tools/generate_power_models.py` already builds
original glTF files with no third-party content, so there is nothing to license or credit
and every faction gets a consistent near-future look (the mapping's own "future art
direction" asks for fictional designs, not real vehicles). Blender is the alternative if it
is installed. This also fixes the shared-model problem (one distinct model per unit).

**Sourced candidates (CC0 unless stated):**
- [OpenGameArt: Heavy Tank \[extra low poly, RTS\]](https://opengameart.org/content/heavy-tankextra-low-poly-rts) - listed CC0, glb/blend. Strongest match for an RTS ground unit.
- [OpenGameArt: Low poly tank assets](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-tank-assets) and [N64 style tank models](https://opengameart.org/content/n64-style-tank-models-and-textures) - listed CC0, very low poly.
- [OpenGameArt: Mini tank](https://opengameart.org/content/mini-tank) - CC0, untextured.
- [OpenGameArt: Abstract RTS Model Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/3d-assets-cc0) and [3D vehicles under CC0](https://opengameart.org/content/3d-vehicles-under-cc0) - CC0 collections including tanks, aircraft, a biplane and a jet.
- [OpenGameArt: Adversary aircraft](https://opengameart.org/content/adversary-aircraft) - CC0, low poly aircraft (author asks for credit though not required).
- [itch.io: Low Poly WWII German Tanks](https://ice-cube-games.itch.io/low-poly-wwii-german-tanks) - CC0, but WWII styling and file format unconfirmed.
- [Kenney Car Kit](https://kenney-assets.itch.io/car-kit) - CC0, 40+ civilian vehicles in glTF; useful for trucks and logistics units, and civilian traffic.
- [Quaternius](https://licenseorg.com/guide/3d-assets/quaternius) - described as CC0 low poly; no military pack found in the search, so browse quaternius.com directly. Already used for the water tower.
- [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza) - already the source of the buildings; filter by CC0 and search tank, helicopter, jet. Nothing confirmed in the search.

**Needs credit (CC BY), allowed by the project rule if credited:**
- [WW2 RTS and SLG Generic Tank Pack](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/winyunq-ww2-rts-slg-generic-tank-pack/2763013) - CC BY 4.0, GLB LODs per vehicle; WWII styling.
- Sketchfab models marked CC BY (as with the three recon planes); check each page. [Military Landvehicle Kit 1.2](https://www.sketchfab.com/3d-models/military-landvehicle-kit-12-cc0-91f495435f624d8b97a768f692aa6ce9) is titled CC0 but Sketchfab treats it as CC BY, so credit it.

**Avoid:** CGTrader, TurboSquid and Unity Asset Store models (custom "royalty free"
licences, often paid, some AI-generated), and anything extracted from a game or mod pack.
Many of these also restrict redistribution inside a source repository.

## Suggested order

1. Stop shipping the donor art: keep the 13 `reference_models` files out of any release
   build until replaced, and delete `nature/trees/oak_tree_*.glb` and the unreferenced
   `cc0/` files after confirming nothing loads them.
2. Give each unit its own model, starting with units that currently share one: Swarm Tank,
   Assault Vehicle, Scout Bike, Scout Car, the three airlifters.
3. Fill the missing units: Patrol Boat, and the Industrial roster's scouts, air and boats
   once the roster work adds them.
4. For every replacement, add the credit entry and checksum in the same commit, and keep
   `test_credits.gd` green.
